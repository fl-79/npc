TIMELINE OVER THE PERIOD
One point a week over the period: hours a week per person, the share of students over the high-stress line, comfort and ties. The band is the spread across seeds.
MORE
HOW The term layer plans every day of the period and takes a snapshot at each week's end; the slider reads one week. WHY A day shows the floor; the period shows the rhythm, the crunch before each review and the recovery after it, which is what the stress rule is calibrated to (about one in five over the line in a normal week, two in five before a review). Each run as a point: contact across (C6, interactions between years), focus up (F1, the focus block). Up and to the right is better on both; a run that gains one and loses the other is a trade.

WHY Collaboration and focus pull against each other in open plans (Bernstein & Turban 2018; Hillier 1996), so a change should be judged on both at once, not one.
