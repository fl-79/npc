NPC V2 · HOW THE SIMULATION WAS MADE

An agent-based model of studio life in UBC's Lasserre Building: the Revit model, what I know of the school and published data, as 176 people living a term.

1THE MODEL
  • Revit 2027 model of the building, with a pyRevit tab for checks and export
  • Exported: 77 rooms, 403 walls, 124 doors, 581 furniture pieces
  • Checked: clashes, slab tops, rooms enclosed
  • Tiled: every level into a 0.5 m grid the agents walk on
  • Site: OpenStreetMap within 320 m
RULEGeometry comes only from the model.
2WHAT I PUT IN
  • Room program: all 77 rooms given a use and their activities
  • 10 model edits: walls, a door, an opening
  • Cohort: 165 students in 11 sections, 11 instructors, 172 desks (7 hot desks)
  • Calendar: Fall 2026, 3 reviews, 4 pin-ups, 5 deadlines from other courses, 3 events, 3 closures
  • Access: the south door on a card 24/7, the others in business hours
  • About 78 answers to a studio-culture checklist, over 6 sessions
  • 34 design questions, 29 settled: washrooms, plotters, crits, lunch, the lift, rearranging

6WHY AGENTS
  • Each person keeps their own week: year, section, crits, a job, a commute. 176 different days at once.
  • Patterns emerge, not drawn in: a plotter queue, a full kitchen at lunch, a talk on a route.
  • One building, many situations: a normal Tuesday, a final's eve, Good Times, a snow day.
  • Changes tried first: agents move a table; two runs are compared.
  • Every choice keeps its reason, so results trace back.
