# Research references — agent simulation of collaborative creative spaces

Links checked 2026-11-03. Items marked *(library)* have no free copy online; borrow them through UBC Library.

## 1. Collaborative work environments (space ↔ interaction evidence)

| Reference | Why it matters for the tool | Link |
|---|---|---|
| Allen, T. — *Managing the Flow of Technology* (MIT Press, 1977/1984). The "Allen curve". | Communication frequency drops sharply with distance (~30 m in Kabo et al.'s citation). A baseline the sim should reproduce. | [Summary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allen_curve) *(book: library)* |
| Kabo, Owen-Smith et al. (2014) "Proximity effects on the dynamics and outcomes of scientific collaborations", *Research Policy* 43(9) | Path-overlap metric: +100 ft of overlap ≈ +20% collaborations. Directly implementable. | [PDF](https://public.websites.umich.edu/~jdos/papers/2014/Proximity_Effects_on_the_Dynamics_and_Outcomes_of_Scientific_Collaboration.pdf) · [UM press](https://news.umich.edu/?p=20932) |
| Bernstein & Turban (2018) "The impact of the 'open' workspace on human collaboration", *Phil. Trans. R. Soc. B* | Face-to-face interaction fell ~70% after an open-plan move. Shows that openness does not mean more collaboration. | [Open access](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6030579) |
| Sailer & Penn (2009) "Spatiality and transpatiality in workplace environments", 7th Space Syntax Symposium | Spatial vs. social (transpatial) drivers of contact. These can be separate agent parameters. | [UCL Discovery](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/3499/) |
| Sailer & Penn, related paper on space and organisation | Movement patterns from layout predict contact frequency. | [UCL Discovery](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/1342930/) |
| Hillier, B. — *Space is the Machine* (free e-edition) | Space syntax theory: integration, visibility, movement. | [UCL Discovery (PDF)](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/3881/) |
| Peponis et al. (2007) "Designing space to support knowledge work", *Environment and Behavior* 39(6) | Uses spatial and network analysis together. A model for your metrics. | [PDF (CORE)](https://core.ac.uk/download/237713056.pdf) · [Academia](https://www.academia.edu/11424908/Designing_space_to_support_knowledge_work) |
| Heerwagen, Kampschroer, Powell, Loftness (2004) "Collaborative knowledge work environments", *Building Research & Information* 32(6) | Review: collaboration = social + solitary work. Supports the focus vs. interruption trade-off. | [Academia](https://www.academia.edu/20012912/Collaborative_knowledge_work_environments) · DOI 10.1080/09613210412331313025 |
| Olguín, Waber, … Pentland (2009) "Sensible Organizations", *IEEE Trans. SMC-B* | Sociometric badges measured face-to-face time, proximity and activity. Real-world counterpart to sim logs. | [MIT tech report PDF](https://hd.media.mit.edu/tech-reports/TR-623-OLD.pdf) · [DSpace](https://dspace.mit.edu/handle/1721.1/62024) |
| Gensler Research Institute — Global Workplace Survey 2024 | Industry data on office use and on effectiveness for focus vs. collaboration. | [Gensler](https://www.gensler.com/press-releases/global-survey-workplace-experience-2024) |
