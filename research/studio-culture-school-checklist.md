# School-specific checklist: studio culture assumptions to confirm

Companion to [summary-studio-culture.md](summary-studio-culture.md) and [summary-metrics.md](summary-metrics.md). Compiled 2026-11-05. External data used to set defaults is logged in [assumption-data-sources.md](assumption-data-sources.md). The generic, reusable name and category for each item are in [input-registry.md](input-registry.md). Factors that can't be quantified are in [subjective-components.md](subjective-components.md). The process is recorded in [methodology-log.md](methodology-log.md).

Every item below is something the simulation needs that **varies by school**, or that the sources either don't quantify or quantify only for a US school.

**How to fill it in:**
- Enter the UBC SALA value in **Your value**. Write "?" if you don't know it, or a range if it varies (e.g. "4–6 h").
- **Reference** is what the literature reports, with its source.
- **Current default** is what I would use if the item is left blank. **[assumed]** means there is no source behind it.
- **Confirm via** suggests where to find the value:
  - **Cal** = UBC Calendar or SALA website
  - **Syl** = course syllabus or outline
  - **Obs** = your own observation
  - **Ask** = ask classmates or instructors (a quick poll works)

Reference sources: Battisto 2024 is one US school (Clemson), Fall 2021. *ST* is *Studio Teaching in Higher Education*. AIAS is the American Institute of Architecture Students reports.
