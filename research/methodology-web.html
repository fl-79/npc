HOW NPC V2 WAS MADE

A simulation of a studio building, made from its Revit model, what the school knows about itself, and published research.

1THE BUILDING
  • The Revit model of the Lasserre Building, floor by floor
  • Rooms, walls, doors and furniture, nothing drawn by hand
  • Turned into a walkable grid the people move on
2WHAT I ADDED

What a model can't say about how a school works:

  • What each room is for and what happens there
  • What objects allow: a kettle, a plotter, a crit wall
  • Who is there: 165 students in three years, 11 instructors
  • The term: studios, crits, reviews, events
3WHAT RESEARCH SAYS
  • Shared routes lead to new collaborations (Kabo 2014)
  • Open plans can cut face-to-face contact (Bernstein & Turban 2018)
  • Focus is broken about every 11 minutes (Mark et al. 2005)
  • How stressed students are in a normal week (UBC graduate survey)

Anything without data behind it is marked as an assumption.

4THE PEOPLE

176 people, each different.

  • Personality: the Big Five traits
  • Rhythm: morning or evening type, a commute, a job
  • Needs: food, rest, company
  • Studio life: a year, a desk, deadlines, crits

Their habits come from their traits, not from a fixed type.

5WHAT IT SHOWS
COLLABORATION
  • Shared routes, new acquaintances, talk across years
FOCUS
  • Uninterrupted work, quiet desks, waits at the plotters

Design suggestions, each traced back to what caused it.
