NPC

NPC simulates how a building is lived in, through the eyes of the many different people who use it, so a design can be tested against their days before it is built. It is a studio project for ARCH 540 at UBC, set in the Frederic Lasserre Building, the home of the School of Architecture and Landscape Architecture.

V2 · NOVEMBER 2026LATEST

The building from its Revit model. 176 students and instructors, each with their own traits, needs and schedule, live a day or a stretch of the Fall 2026 term: crits, plotter queues, the lift, lunch, conversations between years. The tool measures collaboration and focus against goals from research and suggests design changes, each traced to what caused it.
