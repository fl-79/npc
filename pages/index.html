NPC

A CASE STUDY IN CREATIVE WORKSPACES

A design studio is hard to judge from its plan. Hundreds of people in different years, with different schedules, deadlines and habits, use it in patterns that shift by the hour and across a term. NPC takes UBC's Frederic Lasserre Building, home of the School of Architecture and Landscape Architecture, as a case study: it fills the building with agents, each with their own traits and routine, so those complex, varied patterns play out and can be measured, and design changes can be tested before they are built.
